2448!! The resolution function is expressed in terms of the ratio of grid-spacing to deformation radius.
2449!! The square of the resolution parameter is
2450!!
2451!! \f[
2452!! R^2 = \frac{L_d^2}{\Delta^2} = \frac{ c_g^2 }{ f^2 \Delta^2 + c_g \beta \Delta^2 }
2453!! \f]
2454!!
2455!! where the grid spacing is calculated as
2456!!
2457!! \f[
2458!! \Delta^2 = \Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2 .
2459!! \f]
2460!!
2461!! \todo Check this reference to Bob on/off paper.

2463!!
2464!! \f[
2465!! r(\Delta,L_d) = \frac{1}{1+(\alpha R)^p}
