2597!! \section section_gm Isopycnal height diffusion (aka Gent-McWilliams)
2598!!
2599!! Isopycnal height diffusion is implemented via along-layer mass fluxes
2600!! \f[
2601!! h^\dagger \leftarrow h^n - \Delta t \nabla \cdot ( \vec{uh}^* )
2602!! \f]
2603!! where the mass fluxes are cast as the difference in vector streamfunction
2604!!
2605!! \f[
2606!! \vec{uh}^* = \delta_k \vec{\psi} .
2607!! \f]
2608!!
2609!! The GM implementation of isopycnal height diffusion made the streamfunction proportional
2610!! to the potential density slope
2611!! \f[
2612!! \vec{\psi} = - \kappa_h \frac{\nabla_z \rho}{\partial_z \rho}
2613!! = \frac{g\kappa_h}{\rho_o} \frac{\nabla \rho}{N^2} = -\kappa_h \frac{M^2}{N^2}
2614!! \f]
2615!! but for robustness the scheme is implemented as
2616!! \f[
2617!! \vec{\psi} = -\kappa_h \frac{M^2}{\sqrt{N^4 + M^4}}
2618!! \f]
2619!! since the quantity \f$\frac{M^2}{\sqrt{N^4 + M^4}}\f$ is bounded between $-1$ and $1$ and does not change sign
2620!! if \f$N^2<0\f$.
2621!!
2622!! Optionally, the method of \cite ferrari2010, can be used to obtain the streamfunction which solves the
2623!! vertically elliptic equation:
2624!! \f[
2625!! \gamma_F \partial_z c^2 \partial_z \psi - N_*^2 \psi = -( 1 + \gamma_F ) \kappa_h N_*^2 \frac{M^2}{\sqrt{N^4+M^4}}
2626!! \f]
2627!! which recovers the previous streamfunction relation in the limit that \f$ c \rightarrow 0 \f$.
2628!! Here, \f$c=\max(c_{min},c_g)\f$ is the maximum of either \f$c_{min}\f$ and either the first baroclinic mode
2629!! wave-speed or the equivalent barotropic mode wave-speed.
2630!! \f$N_*^2 = \max(N^2,0)\f$ is a non-negative form of the square of the buoyancy frequency.
2631!! The parameter \f$\gamma_F\f$ is used to reduce the vertical smoothing length scale.
2632!! \f[
2633!! \kappa_h = \left( \kappa_o + \alpha_{s} L_{s}^2 < S N > + \alpha_{M} \kappa_{M} \right) r(\Delta x,L_d)
2634!! \f]
2635!! where \f$ S \f$ is the isoneutral slope magnitude, \f$ N \f$ is the buoyancy frequency,
2636!! \f$\kappa_{M}\f$ is the diffusivity calculated by the MEKE parameterization (mom_meke module) and
2637!! \f$ r(\Delta x,L_d) \f$ is a function of the local resolution (ratio of grid-spacing, \f$\Delta x\f$,
2638!! to deformation radius, \f$L_d\f$). The length \f$L_s\f$ is provided by the mom_lateral_mixing_coeffs module
2639!! (enabled with <code>USE_VARIABLE_MIXING=True</code> and the term \f$<SN>\f$ is the vertical average slope
2640!! times the buoyancy frequency prescribed by \cite visbeck1996.
2641!!
2642!! The result of the above expression is subsequently bounded by minimum and maximum values, including an upper
2643!! diffusivity consistent with numerical stability (\f$ \kappa_{cfl} \f$ is calculated internally).
2644!! \f[
2645!! \kappa_h \leftarrow \min{\left( \kappa_{max}, \kappa_{cfl}, \max{\left( \kappa_{min}, \kappa_h \right)} \right)}
2646!! f(c_g,z)
2647!! \f]
2648!!
2649!! where \f$f(c_g,z)\f$ is a vertical structure function.
2650!! \f$f(c_g,z)\f$ is calculated in module mom_lateral_mixing_coeffs.
2651!! If <code>KHTH_USE_EBT_STRUCT=True</code> then \f$f(c_g,z)\f$ is set to look like the equivalent barotropic
2652!! modal velocity structure. Otherwise \f$f(c_g,z)=1\f$ and the diffusivity is independent of depth.
2653!!
2654!! In order to calculate meaningful slopes in vanished layers, temporary copies of the thermodynamic variables
2655!! are passed through a vertical smoother, function vert_fill_ts():
2656!! \f{eqnarray*}{
2657!! \left[ 1 + \Delta t \kappa_{smth} \frac{\partial^2}{\partial_z^2} \right] \theta & \leftarrow & \theta \\
2658!! \left[ 1 + \Delta t \kappa_{smth} \frac{\partial^2}{\partial_z^2} \right] s & \leftarrow & s
2659!! \f}
