1680!!
1681!! This module contains subroutines that apply various diabatic processes. Usually these
1682!! subroutines are called from the MOM_diabatic module. All of these routines use appropriate
1683!! limiters or logic to work properly with arbitrary layer thicknesses (including massless layers)
1684!! and an arbitrarily large timestep.
1685!!
1686!! The subroutine make_frazil facilitates the formation of frazil ice when the ocean water
1687!! drops below the in situ freezing point by heating the water to the freezing point and
1688!! accumulating the required heat for exchange with the sea-ice module.
1689!!
1690!! The subroutine adjust_salt adds salt as necessary to keep the salinity above a
1691!! specified minimum value, and keeps track of the cumulative additions. If the minimum
1692!! salinity is the natural value of 0, this routine should never do anything.
1693!!
1694!! The subroutine differential_diffuse_T_S solves a pair of tridiagonal equations for
1695!! the diffusion of temperatures and salinities with differing diffusivities.
1696!!
1697!! The subroutine triDiagTS solves a tridiagonal equations for the evolution of temperatures
1698!! and salinities due to net entrainment by layers and a diffusion with the same diffusivity.
1699!!
1700!! The subroutine triDiagTS_Eulerian solves a tridiagonal equations for the evolution of
1701!! temperatures and salinities due to diffusion with the same diffusivity, but no net entrainment.
1702!!
1703!! The subroutine find_uv_at_h interpolates velocities to thickness points, optionally also
1704!! using tridiagonal equations to solve for the impacts of net entrainment or mixing of
1705!! momentum between layers.
1706!!
1707!! The subroutine set_pen_shortwave determines the optical properties of the water column and
1708!! the net shortwave fluxes, and stores them in the optics type, working via calls to set_opacity.
1709!!
1710!! The subroutine applyBoundaryFluxesInOut updates the layer thicknesses, temperatures and
1711!! salinities due to the application of the surface forcing. It may also calculate the implied
1712!! turbulent kinetic energy requirements for this forcing to be mixed over the model's finite
1713!! vertical resolution in the surface layers.
