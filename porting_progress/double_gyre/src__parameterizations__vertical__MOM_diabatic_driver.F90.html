4007!!
4008!! This program contains the subroutine that, along with the
4009!! subroutines that it calls, implements diapycnal mass and momentum
4010!! fluxes and a bulk mixed layer. The diapycnal diffusion can be
4011!! used without the bulk mixed layer.
4012!!
4013!! \section section_diabatic Outline of MOM diabatic
4014!!
4015!! * diabatic first determines the (diffusive) diapycnal mass fluxes
4016!! based on the convergence of the buoyancy fluxes within each layer.
4017!!
4018!! * The dual-stream entrainment scheme of MacDougall and Dewar (JPO,
4019!! 1997) is used for combined diapycnal advection and diffusion,
4020!! calculated implicitly and potentially with the Richardson number
4021!! dependent mixing, as described by Hallberg (MWR, 2000).
4022!!
4023!! * Diapycnal advection is the residual of diapycnal diffusion,
4024!! so the fully implicit upwind differencing scheme that is used is
4025!! entirely appropriate.
4026!!
4027!! * The downward buoyancy flux in each layer is determined from
4028!! an implicit calculation based on the previously
4029!! calculated flux of the layer above and an estimated flux in the
4030!! layer below. This flux is subject to the following conditions:
4031!! (1) the flux in the top and bottom layers are set by the boundary
4032!! conditions, and (2) no layer may be driven below a minimal thickness.
4033!! If there is a bulk mixed layer, the buffer layer is treated
4034!! as a fixed density layer with vanishingly small diffusivity.
4035!!
4036!! diabatic takes 5 arguments: the two velocities (u and v), the
4037!! thicknesses (h), a structure containing the forcing fields, and
4038!! the length of time over which to act (dt). The velocities and
4039!! thickness are taken as inputs and modified within the subroutine.
4040!! There is no limit on the time step.
4041
