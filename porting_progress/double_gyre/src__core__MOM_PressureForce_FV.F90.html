2379!!
2380!! Provides the Boussinesq and non-Boussinesq forms of horizontal accelerations
2381!! due to pressure gradients using a vertically integrated finite volume form,
2382!! as described by Adcroft et al., 2008. Integration in the vertical is made
2383!! either by quadrature or analytically.
2384!!
2385!! This form eliminates the thermobaric instabilities that had been a problem with
2386!! previous forms of the pressure gradient force calculation, as described by
2387!! Hallberg, 2005.
2388!!
2389!! Adcroft, A., R. Hallberg, and M. Harrison, 2008: A finite volume discretization
2390!! of the pressure gradient force using analytic integration. Ocean Modelling, 22,
